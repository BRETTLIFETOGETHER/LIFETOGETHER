The problem is not your preaching.

It is Wednesday afternoon. Sunday's message was strong — you felt the room lean in — and by midweek it is already fading under carpools and inboxes and everything else that fills a life. Not because your people don't care. Because a sermon was never meant to carry a church alone.

A campaign closes that gap. The sermon becomes a daily devotional. The devotional becomes a small group conversation. The conversation becomes community, and community — six weeks later — has become movement. Sermon to curriculum, curriculum to community, community to movement. That is the architecture behind the most fruitful churchwide seasons of the last twenty-five years, and it is what every campaign here is built to do.

The lineage behind the library

Brett Eastman built the campaign architecture behind The Purpose Driven Life — the model that helped tens of thousands of churches turn one book into a churchwide season — and has spent twenty-five years refining it with more than five hundred of the largest and fastest-growing churches in America, from Saddleback to Willow Creek and hundreds you have never heard of who simply wanted their people formed. This library is that architecture, opened up: every campaign traces to that same spine of sermon, story, group, and step.

Every objection, answered straight

We've never run a churchwide campaign. Is this too much for us?

Start with the 7-Day Experience. One week, six days, two Sundays. It is designed as a first yes — small enough to run well, big enough to taste what alignment feels like.

My people are tired. Won't this add more?

A campaign subtracts. One message, one arc, every ministry — instead of five departments pulling five directions. Churches consistently report the season felt lighter, not heavier, because everything finally pointed the same way.

How is this different from a sermon series?

A series is heard. A campaign is lived. The difference is the daily devotional in their hands, the group around their table, and the step they take before next Sunday.

We can't afford a big program right now.

Every price on this site is per church, not per person, and includes all four editions with unlimited reproduction for your congregation. The 7-Day starts at $249. Invoice and PO are welcome — it is how most churches buy.

Will it fit our theology and our voice?

The Scripture spine is broadly evangelical and every text is listed before you buy. Sermon builds arrive as outlines to preach in your voice, not scripts to perform. And the Builder can scope a campaign written specifically to your convictions.

What if our groups outlive the campaign?

That is the point. The leader kit is built to convert campaign hosts into ongoing group leaders — the campaign is the front door; community is the house.
